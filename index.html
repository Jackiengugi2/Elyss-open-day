Quick answers

Open Day questions

Does Open Day cost anything?

No. Open Day is free, and there is no obligation to enrol.

Can I bring my child?

Yes, please do. Tell us how many people are coming so we can prepare.

Where is the Lusigetti Campus?

In Kikuyu, Kiambu County. We send directions when we confirm your slot, or call for help finding us.

What if I can’t make 14 November?

Register anyway and say so in the questions box. We’ll arrange a private visit on a day that works for you.
